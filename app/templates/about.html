About SwimTemp

Sea temperature, without the guesswork.

SwimTemp is built around one question: is the water warm enough to swim here?

The beginning

It started with a trip to Tenerife.

We were planning a trip and wanted a simple answer about swimming conditions. The weather information we found made that harder than it should have been.

Air temperature, inland weather stations, and sea temperature are different measurements. For someone deciding whether to get in the water, sea-surface temperature is the number that matters.

The approach

Measure the water near the coast.

SwimTemp uses satellite-derived sea-surface temperature from Copernicus Marine and combines it with marine-condition data such as waves, swell, and current.

The map lets you search a coastline or select a location directly. Readings are presented with their observation date so it is clear when the measurement was made.

The principle

Useful before impressive.

The goal is not to turn ocean data into another complicated weather dashboard. SwimTemp should make the important number obvious, explain where it came from, and behave predictably when data is delayed or unavailable.
